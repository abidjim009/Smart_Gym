Help & Quick FAQ

Does the camera upload or record my video?

No. Pose tracking runs on your device. Only rep counts and numeric scores are stored.

How are injuries protected?

Flagged body parts exclude high-load exercises and adjust Form Coach targets.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Plans and logs stay locally and sync to the backend after login.
